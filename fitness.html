Training Overview

My approach to fitness is built around a five-day training split, with each day dedicated to a specific muscle group: legs on Monday, shoulders on Tuesday, arms on Wednesday, back on Thursday, and chest on Friday. While I stay fairly consistent with my strength training, I haven't done the best job of regularly incorporating core work and cardio. Improving both is one of my current fitness goals.
